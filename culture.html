Milestones

How the company grew.

Each step added a capability rather than a slogan. We only expanded into a new market after we could serve it responsibly from Hong Kong.

Foundation

Company established in Hong Kong

Maitu Co., Limited is registered in Hong Kong with an office in Kwun Tong, building the company around cross-border business advisory as the first desk.

Expansion

Logistics supporting consultation desk

Freight cost structure, route design and documentation support were brought in-house so advisory recommendations could be operationally delivered.

Expansion

Brand planning practice formed

A dedicated brand planning team joined to help trading clients build identities that survive translation, regulation and channel change.

Expansion

General trading operations launched

Sourcing, negotiation and settlement for wholesale and retail programmes moved under one accountable commercial team.

Expansion

E-commerce consultation added

Cross-border channel strategy, localised listings and storefront operations were introduced as a dedicated consulting line.

Platform

Maitu Mobile released to both app stores

Our management app launched on Google Play and the Apple App Store, putting orders, inventory, supply chain tracking and consulting dashboards in the hands of client teams.

Today

Eight business lines, one operating standard

Cross-border business advisory, logistics supporting consultation, brand planning, general trading and e-commerce consultation now run from a single Hong Kong platform, supported by a partner network across more than sixty markets.

Team spirit

One team, eight desks, no silos.

Advisory, logistics, brand and trade specialists sit on the same floor and in the same app. When a shipment is late, the person who answers is the person who moved it — not a ticket queue.

Compliance & responsibility

How we trade, and how we check.

Global compliance

Every engagement is screened against applicable export controls, sanctions restrictions and customs requirements in the jurisdictions we operate. Where a counterparty or a lane cannot be verified, we decline the instruction.

Our CSR commitments

We work with partners who meet our labour, safety and environmental standards, publish our data handling practice openly, and apply age-appropriate design to every product we distribute.

The detail lives in our legal documents: how we handle personal data across our website and mobile apps is set out in the Privacy Policy, and the rules that govern your use of our services and the Maitu app are set out in the Terms of Service.
