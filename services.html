Questions

Frequently asked.

Which markets do you operate in?

We are headquartered in Kwun Tong, Hong Kong and run engagements across Greater China, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe and North America.

Can we engage a single business line?

Yes. Most clients start with one line — typically advisory or logistics — and expand once the operating rhythm is proven.

Is the mobile app included?

Maitu Mobile is included with every managed engagement. Client accounts are provisioned on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

How do you handle compliance and customs?

Documentation, licensing and customs liaison sit inside the engagement, and every movement is recorded against your own compliance requirements.

Do you work with existing suppliers?

Always. We typically strengthen the supplier base you already have, and source new partners only where a measurable gap exists.

How does pricing work?

Fixed-scope advisory engagements and rolling managed-service contracts, quoted after the discovery step so the numbers are comparable.
